METHOD (from the paper, to implement from spec):
  - Threat model: inference-time reasoning (CoT) backdoors — a malicious reasoning path is
    injected at inference without changing weights. Black-box on output probabilities;
    no reference set and no trigger needed (fits our runtime threat model).
  - Signal: a malicious token path shows HIGH POSTERIOR probability despite a LOW PRIOR
    probability under the model's general knowledge (the "state-transition amplification
    ratio"). Persistent anomalies are flagged with CUSUM change-point detection.
  - Claimed results: AUROC ~1.0 across 8B-70B on five datasets, ~42x more efficient than
    baselines, robust to adaptive attacks (authors' own claims, unverified).
  - Why we want it: change-point detection is a different mechanism than ConfGuard's
    confidence-lock, so it may catch suppressed payloads where the lock inverts; built for
    the agentic/reasoning setting that ConfGuard and the activation monitors struggle on
    (Watch-QA / Watch-OA).
